## Test data

The GRIB2 messages in `fixtures/` (and the `.idx` inventory there) are unmodified messages from the NOAA National Blend of
Models, retrieved from the open data bucket. See https://registry.opendata.aws/noaa-nbm/ for the dataset and its terms of use.
Files in `test/data/` are synthetic and generated by the scripts beside them.
